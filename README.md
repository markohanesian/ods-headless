## Brand Tone & Copywriting Guidelines

All page copy and content across Ohanesian Digital Solutions (ODS) should follow these core copywriting standards:

- **Voice & Tone**: ROI-driven, authoritative, problem-solving, and clear. Speak directly to business owners who want to eliminate paperwork, capture qualified leads, and automate operational friction.
- **Hero & Headline Hook**: Lead with high-impact outcomes (e.g. *"Turn Your Website into Your Hardest-Working Sales Rep"*).
- **Comparative Positioning**: Clearly contrast standard off-the-shelf builders (Wix / WordPress / templates) against custom web engineering built for strategy and speed.
- **Service Details**: Detail specific, actionable deliverables ("What You Get") rather than generic summaries.
- **CTAs**: Use compelling, specific calls-to-action such as *"Get Your Free Site Audit"*, *"Start Your Free 2-Minute Assessment"*, and *"Claim Your Free Strategy Call"*.
