# ODS Copywriting & Brand Messaging Rules

When writing or modifying website copy across Ohanesian Digital Solutions (ODS) digital properties, adhere to the following copy standards:

1. **Tone & Voice Principles**:
   - **Direct & ROI-Focused**: Focus on business outcomes, lead conversion, automated workflows, and operational time saved (e.g., "Turn Your Website into Your Hardest-Working Sales Rep").
   - **Technical Mastery Without Jargon**: Communicate senior software engineering authority, WCAG compliance, speed, and custom web architecture cleanly without alienating non-technical decision-makers.
   - **Problem / Solution Contrast**: Contrast off-the-shelf builder friction (fragile plugins, slow loads, messy emails) directly against ODS custom engineering (instant loads, automated intake, zero bloat).

2. **Core Messaging Pillars**:
   - **Performance & Speed**: Instant mobile/desktop page loads that retain visitors and boost conversion.
   - **Operational Automation**: Multi-step smart intake forms, lead screening, payment collection, and booking automation.
   - **Accessibility & Compliance**: 100% WCAG-compliant design and accessible user experiences for all users.
   - **Ongoing Peace of Mind**: Webmaster Care Plan managing hosting, security, and updates 24/7.

3. **Page Copy Standards**:
   - Maintain clear H1/H2 hierarchy on every page.
   - Action-oriented CTAs ("Get Your Free Site Audit", "Start Your Free 2-Minute Assessment", "Claim Your Free Strategy Call").
   - Detailed "What You Get" breakdowns for all service tiers.
